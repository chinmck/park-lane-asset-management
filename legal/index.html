Regulatory status

Parklane Asset Management Ltd is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). We do not carry on any activity that requires FCA authorisation. In particular, we do not:

  • give investment, financial, tax or legal advice;
  • manage investments, securities, shares or other financial instruments for anyone;
  • arrange or deal in investments for others;
  • operate a collective investment scheme;
  • accept deposits; or
  • offer shares, bonds, loan notes or other securities through this website or otherwise to the public.

Because Parklane is not FCA-authorised, you would not have access to the Financial Ombudsman Service or the Financial Services Compensation Scheme in relation to any dealings with us.
