What Parklane does

Parklane acquires, owns and manages residential property in the United Kingdom, mainly houses in multiple occupation and room-let homes in Liverpool, Wirral and the North West, on its own account. Our website describes our approach and allows people to request an introduction.

Regulatory status

Parklane Asset Management Ltd is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). We do not carry on any activity that requires FCA authorisation. In particular, we do not: - give investment, financial, tax or legal advice; - manage investments, securities, shares or other financial instruments for anyone; - arrange or deal in investments for others; - operate a collective investment scheme; - accept deposits; or - offer shares, bonds, loan notes or other securities through this website or otherwise to the public.

Because Parklane is not FCA-authorised, you would not have access to the Financial Ombudsman Service or the Financial Services Compensation Scheme in relation to any dealings with us.

Property risk

Property values and rental income can go down as well as up. Property is relatively illiquid and can take time to sell. Changes in law, licensing, planning, taxation, interest rates and local markets can affect property. References on this website to long-term ownership, value or growth describe our approach. They are not promises, forecasts or indications of any return.
